# Context Preset: Marketplace

## Tone
Conversational, direct, concise, and local.

## Rules
- Normal greetings, thanks, and scheduling language are welcome (`Thanks for picking it up!`, `Sounds good. See you at 5.`, `Sorry, I can't make that time.`).
- Genuine selling points may be stated clearly, but avoid unsupported superlatives ("best deal in town", "guaranteed flawless") or manipulative urgency.
- Price negotiation: state figures, conditions, and pickup boundaries directly without apologetic softening.
- Genuine offers of cognitive or physical assistance are welcome (`我可以帮你搬下楼`, `我可以提供两款推车的尺寸对比`).
- Role prompts may specialize phrasing, but may never introduce scripted service theater.
